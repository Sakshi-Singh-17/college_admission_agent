1. GENERAL ADMISSION OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Greenfield University (GFU) is committed to a transparent, merit-based, and inclusive
admissions process. Applications are accepted for Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG),
and Doctoral programs. All applicants must meet minimum eligibility criteria and submit
complete applications by the stated deadlines.

2. UNDERGRADUATE ADMISSION POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 Eligibility
  - Completion of 10+2 (or equivalent) from a recognised board with a minimum aggregate
    of 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/OBC-NCL candidates).
  - Science stream required for B.Tech, B.Sc programs.
  - Commerce stream required for B.Com, BBA programs.
  - Any stream accepted for BA, BFA, and BDes programs.

2.2 Entrance Examinations
  - Engineering (B.Tech): JEE Main score mandatory; JEE Advanced required for
    specialised tracks. Minimum JEE Main percentile: 70.
  - Management (BBA): GFU Management Aptitude Test (GMAT-U) or equivalent state CET.
  - Science (B.Sc): GFU Science Entrance Test (GSET) or 12th board merit.
  - Arts & Design (BA/BFA/BDes): Portfolio submission + personal interview.

2.3 Selection Criteria
  Admission is based on a composite score:
    40% — Entrance exam score
    40% — 10+2 board percentage
    10% — Extracurricular achievements (verified certificates required)
    10% — Personal interview / portfolio (programme-dependent)

2.4 Reservations (as per Government of India norms)
  SC: 15% | ST: 7.5% | OBC-NCL: 27% | EWS: 10% | PwD: 5% (horizontal)

3. POSTGRADUATE ADMISSION POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1 Eligibility
  - Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years) from a recognised university with ≥55% aggregate
    (50% for reserved categories).
  - Relevant undergraduate discipline required for most PG programmes (see Course Guide).

3.2 Entrance Examinations
  - M.Tech: GATE score (minimum 450 out of 1000). Direct admission for GATE rank ≤ 500.
  - MBA: CAT / MAT / XAT / GMAT score. Minimum CAT percentile: 75.
  - M.Sc: GFU Postgraduate Entrance Test (GPET) or CSIR-NET/JEST.
  - MA: GFU Arts Entrance (GAET) + Statement of Purpose (SOP).
  - MCA: BCA/B.Sc (CS/IT) graduates preferred; GFU MCA Entrance Test required.

3.3 Selection Process
  Shortlisted candidates appear for:
    - Group Discussion (GD) — MBA programmes only.
    - Written Ability Test (WAT) — MBA and MA programmes.
    - Personal Interview (PI) — all PG programmes.

4. DOCTORAL (Ph.D.) ADMISSION POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1 Eligibility
  - Master's degree with ≥55% aggregate or CGPA 5.5/10 (50% for SC/ST/PwD).
  - Valid NET/JRF/GATE/UGC fellowship preferred; direct admission possible for
    candidates with prior peer-reviewed publications.

4.2 Process
  - Submit Research Proposal (1500–2500 words) with application.
  - Shortlisted candidates appear for a Research Aptitude Test (RAT) followed by
    an interview with the departmental committee.
  - Admission is offered based on research fit, proposal quality, and supervisor
    availability.

5. INTERNATIONAL STUDENT ADMISSIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  - Applications submitted through the International Students Office (ISO).
  - English proficiency: IELTS ≥ 6.5 or TOEFL iBT ≥ 90 (waived for students from
    English-medium institutions).
  - Credential evaluation by Association of Indian Universities (AIU) required for
    non-Indian qualifications.
  - Dedicated 15% supernumerary seats available for NRI/OCI/PIO/Foreign nationals.
  - Visa support and on-campus accommodation guaranteed for the first year.

6. LATERAL ENTRY ADMISSIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  - Diploma holders (3-year polytechnic) may apply directly to the 2nd year of B.Tech
    programmes, subject to seat availability and merit.
  - Minimum 60% in diploma required. Lateral entry slots: 10% of sanctioned intake.
  - No lateral entry for MBA, M.Sc, or Doctoral programmes.

7. CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Withdrawal before last date of 1st merit list  : 100% refund (less processing fee ₹1,000)
  Withdrawal before last date of 2nd merit list  : 80% refund
  Withdrawal before commencement of classes      : 50% refund
  Withdrawal after commencement of classes       : No refund (except security deposit)
  Security deposit (₹5,000) is always refundable upon return of identity card.

8. DOCUMENT VERIFICATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Required documents at the time of admission:
  1. Completed application form (online, printed and signed)
  2. 10th and 12th mark sheets and certificates (originals for verification)
  3. Entrance exam scorecard
  4. Category certificate (if applicable) — issued by competent authority
  5. Transfer/Migration certificate from last institution
  6. Character certificate (original)
  7. 6 passport-size photographs (white background)
  8. Aadhaar card / valid government photo ID
  9. Bank account details (for scholarship processing)
  10. Medical fitness certificate (for certain programmes)
