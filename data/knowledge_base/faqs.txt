SECTION A: APPLICATION PROCESS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q1: How do I apply to Greenfield University?
A: All applications must be submitted through the official online admission portal at
   admissions.greenfielduniversity.edu.in. Create an account, fill the application form,
   upload required documents, and pay the non-refundable application fee of ₹1,000.
   Paper/offline applications are not accepted.

Q2: Can I apply to multiple programmes simultaneously?
A: Yes, you can apply to up to 3 programmes in a single application. Each additional
   programme beyond the first costs ₹500 extra. If you are offered seats in more than
   one programme, you must choose only one within the stipulated deadline.

Q3: Is there an application fee? Is it refundable?
A: The application processing fee is ₹1,000 for the first choice and ₹500 per additional
   programme. This fee is non-refundable under all circumstances.

Q4: What documents are needed for the online application?
A: Scanned copies (PDF/JPG) of: 10th certificate, 12th mark sheet, entrance scorecard,
   category certificate (if applicable), passport-size photograph, and government photo ID.
   Originals are verified only at the time of physical document verification.

Q5: My 12th board results are awaited. Can I still apply?
A: Yes. Candidates awaiting 12th results may apply provisionally. You must upload your
   12th admit card and your 11th-year marks. Final admission is confirmed only after you
   submit the 12th mark sheet and meet the minimum eligibility. Failing to meet
   eligibility will result in cancellation of provisional admission.

SECTION B: ELIGIBILITY & ENTRANCE EXAMS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q6: I scored 48% in 12th. Am I eligible for B.Tech?
A: The minimum for B.Tech (General category) is 60%. With 48%, you do not meet the
   eligibility for B.Tech. You may be eligible for B.Sc or BA programmes (50% minimum).
   SC/ST/OBC candidates have a 5% relaxation — so the minimum for them is 55% for B.Tech.

Q7: Which entrance exam do I need for B.Tech admission?
A: JEE Main is mandatory for all B.Tech programmes. The minimum JEE Main percentile
   required is 70 (General/EWS). For B.Tech CSE (AI/ML) and Cybersecurity, a minimum
   of 80 percentile is required. JEE Advanced score is used for merit-based scholarships
   but is not mandatory for admission.

Q8: I have a CAT score. Can I apply for MBA at GFU?
A: Yes. GFU accepts CAT, MAT, XAT, and GMAT scores for MBA admissions. A minimum
   CAT percentile of 75 is required. After clearing the score threshold, shortlisted
   candidates appear for GD, WAT, and Personal Interview rounds.

Q9: Is there an age limit for admissions?
A: There is no upper age limit for any programme at GFU. However, for UG admissions,
   candidates must have passed 12th by the year of admission.

Q10: I have a gap year after 12th. Does that affect my eligibility?
A: A gap year does not disqualify you. You must clearly state the reason for the gap
    in your application. There is no penalty for one or two gap years.

Q11: What is the difference between B.Tech CSE and B.Tech CSE (AI/ML)?
A: B.Tech CSE is a broad computer science programme covering algorithms, OS, databases,
    networks, and software engineering. CSE (AI/ML) is a specialised track with a deeper
    focus on machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, NLP, and AI ethics from
    the first year. CSE (AI/ML) has a higher JEE cutoff and slightly higher fees.

Q12: Can I switch my specialisation after joining?
A: Specialisation is chosen in the 5th semester (3rd year). Students can switch within
    the same school subject to seat availability and a minimum CGPA of 7.0 after 4 semesters.
    Switching between schools (e.g., Engineering to Management) is not permitted.

Q13: Does GFU offer dual-degree programmes?
A: Yes. B.Tech + MBA (5-year integrated) is available for top-ranked B.Tech applicants
    (JEE percentile ≥ 95). Students may also enrol in a Minor programme (18 credits)
    from any other school alongside their major.

Q14: What career support does GFU provide?
A: GFU has a dedicated Career Development Centre (CDC) that offers placement training,
    mock interviews, resume workshops, and on-campus recruitment. Average placement
    rates: Engineering 92%, MBA 96%, Science 78%. Top recruiters include Google, Amazon,
    Infosys, HDFC Bank, McKinsey, Deloitte, and DRDO.

SECTION D: FEES, SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL AID
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q15: Are there any scholarships available for meritorious students?
A: Yes. GFU offers merit scholarships ranging from 25% to 100% tuition fee waiver for
    top-performing students. JEE Main ≥ 99 percentile earns a 75% waiver. Board toppers
    (state rank 1) get a full waiver. Need-based scholarships are also available for
    families with annual income below ₹5 lakh. Refer to the Fee Structure document for
    detailed scholarship criteria.

Q16: Can I pay the fee in instalments?
A: Yes. The annual fee is split into two equal instalments — 50% at the time of admission
    and 50% at the start of Semester 2. GFU also facilitates education loans through
    SBI, Canara Bank, and HDFC Bank.

Q17: What is included in the tuition fee?
A: Tuition fee covers academic instruction, library access, laboratory usage, and
    one set of academic materials. It does NOT cover hostel, mess, exam fees, or
    extracurricular activity charges.

SECTION E: HOSTEL & CAMPUS FACILITIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q18: Is hostel accommodation guaranteed for all students?
A: Hostel is guaranteed for all first-year students who apply by 31 July 2025.
    Returning students must apply by 30 June. There are separate hostels for boys
    and girls. Hostel allotment is based on distance from campus and merit.

Q19: What facilities does the campus offer?
A: GFU campus (85 acres) includes: central library (1.2 lakh books + digital access),
    sports complex (gym, swimming pool, cricket ground, basketball/tennis courts),
    medical centre, innovation lab, entrepreneurship cell, cafeteria, ATM, and bank branch.
    24×7 Wi-Fi is available across campus.

Q20: Is there transport from the city to campus?
A: GFU operates shuttle buses from Pune Railway Station, Pune Airport, and Hinjewadi
    IT Park. Annual transport pass: ₹15,000. Day-scholar students may also avail
    daily transport at ₹1,500/month.

Q22: I submitted my application but haven't received a confirmation email.
A:  Check your spam/junk folder first. If not found, log in to the portal — the
    application status will show "Submitted". Contact admissions@greenfielduniversity.edu.in
    with your application number if the issue persists.

Q23: Can I update my application after submission?
A:  Minor corrections (phone number, address) are allowed until the application deadline.
    Programme choice and academic details cannot be changed after submission.
    Contact the Admissions Office with a written request for any correction.
