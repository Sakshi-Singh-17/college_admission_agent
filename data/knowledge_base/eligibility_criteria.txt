  NOT eligible for: B.Tech, B.Sc (Science), BCA, BBA Business Analytics

5. SPECIAL CATEGORY ELIGIBILITY NOTES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Persons with Disabilities (PwD):
  - 5% horizontal reservation across all categories.
  - Minimum benchmark disability: 40% as certified by government hospital.
  - Extra time: 20 minutes per hour in entrance exams (GFU-administered).
  - Scribe facility available on request.

Ex-Servicemen / Defence Personnel (Dependents):
  - 2% supernumerary seats per programme.
  - 5% relaxation in minimum percentage criteria.
  - Certificate from Commanding Officer or Discharge Book required.

Kashmiri Migrants:
  - 2 supernumerary seats per programme.
  - Percentage relaxation as per UGC norms.
  - Kashmiri Migrant certificate from state government required.

6. POST-ADMISSION ACADEMIC REQUIREMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  - Minimum 75% attendance per course to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
  - Minimum CGPA of 5.0 required to continue to the next academic year.
  - Students with CGPA below 5.0 after Year 1 will be placed on academic probation;
    below 4.0 may result in withdrawal from the programme.
  - Repeat/Back exams available for students who fail (max 3 attempts per course).
