SCHOLARSHIPS & FEE WAIVERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Merit Scholarships (first-year admission):
  Rank 1 in JEE Main / Board (State Topper) : 100% tuition fee waiver
  JEE Main percentile ≥ 99 / Board ≥ 95%   : 75% tuition fee waiver
  JEE Main percentile 95–98.99 / Board 90–94%: 50% tuition fee waiver

Need-based Scholarships:
  Family income < ₹3 lakh/year (SC/ST/OBC)  : 100% fee waiver
  Family income < ₹5 lakh/year (General/EWS) : 50% fee waiver
  Documents required: Income certificate, caste certificate, Aadhaar

Sports & Cultural Scholarships:
  National/International level achievers     : Up to 50% fee waiver
  State level achievers                      : Up to 25% fee waiver

GFU Alumni Ward Scholarship:
  Children of GFU alumni                    : 10% discount on tuition fee

Education Loan Facilitation:
  GFU is empanelled with SBI, Canara Bank, and HDFC Bank for
  education loans up to ₹15 lakh (UG) and ₹25 lakh (PG) at
  preferential interest rates. Loan support desk: Room 105, Admin Block.
